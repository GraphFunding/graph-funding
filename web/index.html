How a decision is made

  1. 1

    Enlist

    Lock $GRAPHFUND. Your chance of being drawn is your share of everything locked.

  2. 2

    Drawn

    The hash of a future block picks five Keepers for every fork. Nobody knows who in advance.

  3. 3

    Vote

    Each one proposes the split below their fork. The median wins, so one bad vote moves nothing.

  4. 4

    Flow

    Every six hours the money climbs down the tree. Keepers who voted on a fork share 2% of what flows through it.

The rules, on the wall

1 · Where the money comes from

  1. The market. Every $GRAPHFUND trade on Stockereum pays 2%.
  2. The creator wallet. 1% waits in its escrow.
  3. The tree. It is claimed straight into Graph Funding with claim(WETH, GraphFund).
Waiting in the escrownot launched yet

2 · The owner

Can

  • Edit the tree and the starting weights
  • Change epoch length, committee size, voter pay
  • Reject spam proposals

Cannot

  • Touch anyone's stake
  • Touch money owed to a project
  • Choose who gets drawn

The owner renounces after 28 epochs (7 days) with committees drawn and no emergency changes.

4 · Before you lock

Can a whale buy a committee?
Seats are drawn in proportion to what is locked, so whoever locks most of the $GRAPHFUND wins most seats. The median protects a fork's split only while no one holds three of its five seats. Every seat and every vote is public.
Keepers keep 2% of what?
Each epoch, the Keepers who voted on a fork share 2% of the ETH that flowed through that fork. The other 98% keeps climbing down the tree.
Did these projects sign up?
No. They are recipients, not partners. Graph Funding pays the donation address each project publishes; none of them has endorsed it.
Is it audited?
Not yet. The code is open source and tested. The draw uses the hash of a future block, which that block's proposer could bias.
